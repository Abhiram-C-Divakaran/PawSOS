How PawReach Works

From one report to a second chance.

1

Report

Citizen uploads a photo, shares their location, and describes the animal's basic condition.

2

Assess

AI-assisted and rule-based triage helps determine urgency and assigns a priority category.

3

Mobilize

Nearby rescuers, ambulances and veterinary facilities are identified and notified instantly.

4

Recover

Track treatment, foster care, recovery, release or adoption — until the case is fully closed.
